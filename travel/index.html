Tokyo, Day 2 — Old Tokyo, at walking pace

  • 9:00 — Asakusa: Senso-ji temple and Nakamise shopping street, before the midday crowds

  • 14:30 — Ueno Park and its museums — pick one, not three
  • 17:30 — Yanaka old-town streets for a slow evening walk and dinner
