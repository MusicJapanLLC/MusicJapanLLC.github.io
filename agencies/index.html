FAQ

What does white-label mean here?

The system, the demo and the client-facing materials carry your agency's name — not ours. Your clients see you as the provider. We work invisibly in the background.

Do you ever contact my clients?

No. You own the relationship and the billing. We do not market to, invoice, or directly contact your clients.

What can I charge my clients?

Whatever you decide. The typical market range is $299–$497/month with $1,500–$3,000 setup. Our own direct pricing ($497 pilot, then $1,497 setup + $497/month) is a useful anchor.

What do I pay you?

A wholesale setup fee plus a wholesale monthly fee per active client, below our direct prices. Your exact rates are in the partner sheet you receive when you apply — they vary by volume, which is why we don't post a single public number.

How fast can I show this to a client?

Your branded demo is ready within 48 hours. Full client setup takes 3–5 business days.

Which tools does it run on?

Proven platforms such as GoHighLevel and Twilio, plus modern voice/chat AI. It works with your client's existing number and calendar.

What if my client cancels?

Wholesale billing for that client stops when they stop. No long-term contract on the pilot, and no paying for seats you aren't using.
